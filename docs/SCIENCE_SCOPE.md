# Science scope and literature guardrails

The project centres on resolved interstellar-medium science: molecular gas,
star-formation efficiency, variation across galaxies, and environmental and dynamical effects.

Do not claim as new:

- the resolved molecular Schmidt-Kennicutt relation;
- galaxy-to-galaxy variation in resolved SFE;
- that SFE contributes to resolved star-formation scatter;
- that orbital/dynamical quantities can affect depletion time.

The pilot demonstrates a reproducible workflow that can combine matched
molecular-gas, SFR and IFU products and add dynamical predictors. It is
designed to transfer to KILOGAS.

## Research workflow

The pilot combines public molecular-gas and resolved star-formation products
with optical-IFU dynamical diagnostics using matched apertures and explicit
uncertainty tests. The workflow is designed to transfer to KILOGAS while
preserving the provenance and limitations of each input.

Do not claim raw ALMA/JCMT reduction experience unless raw calibration and
imaging are actually performed.
